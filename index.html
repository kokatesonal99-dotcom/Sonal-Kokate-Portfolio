07 · Independent conceptCampaign thinking

A tiny pause for a very loud day.

A made-up campaign for a neighbourhood chai counter: turn the familiar tea break into a playful, shareable reset.

Open the concept
Self-initiated practice case · not a client project

Insight: People already reach for chai in the middle of a hectic day. The ritual is real; the brand can give it a line worth passing around.

Idea: “Chai before chaos.” A short-form series of recognisable workday micro-crises, each interrupted by the first sip.

  • Reels: 6-second chaos-to-calm transitions.
  • Stories: vote on the day’s “chai emergency”.
  • At-counter line: “Take five. Take chai.”
  • Measure saves, shares, store visits and offer redemptions—not views alone.
All brand, audience and campaign details are fictional. No assets have been published and no performance outcomes are claimed.
